3.2 RETAINAGE
    A. The Owner shall withhold ten percent (10%) of the value of completed
       work from each progress payment.
    B. No retainage shall be withheld on materials suitably stored, provided
       the requirements of paragraph 3.4 are satisfied in full.

3.4 MATERIALS STORED OFF SITE
    A. Payment for materials stored off the site shall be made only upon
       submission of all of the following: a paid invoice or bill of sale
       evidencing transfer of title to the Owner; a certificate of insurance
       naming the Owner as an additional insured for the full value of the
       stored material; and the Owner's prior written consent to the storage
       location, including a right of access.

3.6 CHANGE ORDERS
    A. Only Change Orders executed by the Owner and the Contractor shall be
       included in the Contract Sum or billed on the Continuation Sheet.
